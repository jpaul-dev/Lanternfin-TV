YOUR LIBRARY, WITH YOU

Backup and restore

Back up remembered sources, favorites, viewing progress and preferences. Catalog caches and video are not included. Backups stay on this device until you save or copy them.

Create a backup

Choose a separate passphrase of at least 12 characters. It encrypts the backup, including saved provider passwords. Keep it safe; it cannot be recovered.

Restore a backup

Choose a backup file where supported, or paste encrypted backup text with a keyboard. Restored sources are saved to this TV’s unencrypted app storage.

Existing sources keep their names and settings. Favorites and watched marks combine; the latest 100 history entries remain. Current season choices take priority. The app reopens after restore.
