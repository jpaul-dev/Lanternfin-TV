A FRESH START

Reset Lanternfin

Remove saved data from this app on this TV. This cannot be undone. Save an encrypted backup first if you want to restore your sources and viewing library.

What will be removed

All saved sources and sign-in details, favorites, watchlist, viewing history and progress, watched marks, guide matches and corrections, Home layouts, browsing choices, saved catalogs and app preferences.

Your provider account and other TV apps are unaffected. Backups you have already saved elsewhere are kept.

KEEP YOUR PLAYER CURRENT

App updates

Update a developer install

  1. Back up saved sources and viewing progress in Settings.
  2. On your computer, open the Lanternfin fork in GitHub Desktop. Choose the TV branch, fetch changes and review before pulling.
  3. Open Lanternfin TV Setup from that same folder. Build the current checkout; for Samsung, sign it with your existing TV certificate and device permission.
  4. Reinstall the app on your paired TV, then check this screen’s build revision. Keep the app ID and signing identity unchanged.

Rebuilding uses the files already on your computer. It does not fetch new source. An unsigned Samsung archive is not ready to install.

Keep a way back

Keep the previous working installer and your encrypted backup before testing an update. Downloads are local to this TV and are not backed up. A reinstall, developer-session expiry or clearing app data may remove local data.

This developer build cannot silently replace itself. Store distribution and a signed update channel need separate vendor approval and release testing. Matching the source revision is not a claim of hardware certification.

YOUR LIBRARY, WITH YOU

Backup and restore

Back up remembered sources, favorites, watchlist, viewing progress and preferences. Catalog caches and video are not included. Backups stay on this device until you save or copy them.

Create a backup

Choose a separate passphrase of at least 12 characters. It encrypts the backup, including saved provider passwords. Keep it safe; it cannot be recovered.

Restore a backup

Choose a backup file where supported, or paste encrypted backup text with a keyboard. Restored sources are saved to this TV’s unencrypted app storage.

Existing sources keep their names and settings. Favorites, watchlist and watched marks combine; the latest 100 history entries remain. Current season choices, source layouts and guide matches take priority. The app reopens after restore.

Device & playback diagnostics

Device reports are clues, not a playback guarantee. LG’s simulator cannot test DRM. A real TV and authorized provider access are needed to verify protected streams.

Reports cover example codec profiles. Other profiles, audio formats or resolutions may behave differently. Samsung AVPlay has separate native capabilities.

Protected playback check

Check Widevine, PlayReady and Clear Key on this device. Your TV may ask for permission. This checks H.264/AAC in MP4 with temporary licenses and no persistent state or device identifiers. Other codecs, security levels and native players can differ.

The app creates no keys or license sessions and sends no provider requests. Acceptance is not proof that a protected stream will play.
